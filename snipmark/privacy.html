SnipMark Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

SnipMark is a screenshot and annotation app for macOS by xinjing. In short: SnipMark collects no data. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you capture or set leaves your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

  • Screenshots. SnipMark reads the screen only after you start a capture, with the Screen & System Audio Recording permission you grant in System Settings. A screenshot goes only where you send it: the clipboard, or a folder on your Mac (Pictures › SnipMark unless you choose another).
  • Settings. Your shortcuts, save folder and other preferences are stored on your Mac, in SnipMark’s own sandbox container.

What SnipMark doesn’t do

SnipMark has no network access. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it has no accounts. We don’t receive, sell or share any information about you.

Crash reports from Apple

If you chose to share analytics with app developers (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share with app developers), Apple may give us crash reports and anonymous usage statistics about SnipMark. Apple’s privacy policy covers that information; we use it only to fix problems.

Children

SnipMark collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@xinjing.app.
