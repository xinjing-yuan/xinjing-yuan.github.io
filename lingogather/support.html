LingoGather Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@xinjing.app. Mentioning your macOS version and LingoGather’s version (LingoGather › About LingoGather) helps us find the cause sooner.

Getting started

  1. Drag a video or audio file onto the player. Subtitle files with the same name in the same folder load automatically.
  2. No subtitles? Choose Subtitles › Generate Subtitles to create them on your Mac, or Subtitles › Search Subtitles Online….
  3. Open the notes window to choose a folder for your notes, then save sentences, words and highlights into notes and flashcards as you learn.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. LingoGather has no accounts. Cloud translation, AI chat, cloud speech recognition and AI voices are optional: each uses your own API key from that provider, and the provider bills you directly. Settings › API Key lists every service with a link to where you get its key.

What works without an internet connection?

Playback, shadowing, notes, flashcards and the PDF reader. Generating subtitles on your Mac works offline once you’ve downloaded a speech model, and Apple Translation works offline once you’ve downloaded its languages.

Why does LingoGather ask for the microphone?

To record your shadowing practice, and for dictation and voice chat. Recordings are saved on your Mac; audio is sent out only when you use a cloud service you’ve set up, such as pronunciation feedback or voice chat. You can change this in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

The subtitle file next to my video doesn’t show up.

macOS only lets App Store apps look inside folders you’ve given them. LingoGather can see the Movies, Music and Downloads folders by itself; for videos elsewhere, add the subtitle file with File › Add Subtitles (⌥⌘O), or keep your videos in one of those folders.

Can it play MKV, WebM and other formats?

Yes. When macOS can’t play a file directly, LingoGather converts it on your Mac the first time you open it, which can take a moment for long videos. Your original file isn’t changed.

How do I sync between my Macs?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each Mac. Notes, flashcards, highlights, settings and more sync automatically through your private iCloud; PDFs and media you choose to sync also need iCloud Drive. We can’t see any of it.

Where are on-device models downloaded from?

Speech and language models are downloaded from Hugging Face the first time you use them (a few hundred MB each). If Hugging Face is slow or blocked where you are, choose the mirror in the same settings.

How do I remove LingoGather?

Quit it and move LingoGather from the Applications folder to the Trash. Your notes folder is yours and stays where it is.

System requirements

macOS 15.6 or later. Apple’s built-in speech recognition needs macOS 26 or later; on-device language models need a Mac with Apple silicon.

常見問題

需要註冊帳號或訂閱嗎？

不需要。LingoGather 沒有帳號。雲端翻譯、AI 對話、雲端語音辨識和 AI 朗讀都是選用功能：各自使用你向該服務申請的 API 金鑰，費用由該服務直接向你收取。「設定」›「API Key」列出了每個服務，並附有申請金鑰的連結。

沒有網路時能用哪些功能？

播放、跟讀、筆記、閃卡和 PDF 閱讀。下載過語音模型之後，在 Mac 上產生字幕不需要網路；下載過語言之後，Apple 翻譯也能離線使用。

為什麼 LingoGather 要使用麥克風？

用來錄製你的跟讀，以及聽寫和語音對話。錄音存在你的 Mac 上；只有在你使用自己設定的雲端服務（例如發音回饋、語音對話）時，聲音才會傳出去。可以在「系統設定」›「隱私權與安全性」›「麥克風」更改。

影片旁邊的字幕檔沒有出現？

macOS 只允許 App Store 的 App 讀取你授權過的檔案夾。LingoGather 自己就能看到「影片」、「音樂」和「下載項目」檔案夾；影片放在其他位置時，請用「檔案」›「加入字幕」（⌥⌘O）手動加入字幕檔，或把影片放進上述檔案夾。

能播放 MKV、WebM 等格式嗎？

可以。macOS 無法直接播放的檔案，LingoGather 會在第一次打開時於你的 Mac 上轉換，長影片可能要等一下。原始檔案不會被修改。

如何在多台 Mac 之間同步？

在每台 Mac 上登入同一個 Apple 帳號即可。筆記、閃卡、重點、設定等會透過你私人的 iCloud 自動同步；你選擇同步的 PDF 和媒體檔還需要打開 iCloud 雲碟。我們看不到這些內容。

裝置端模型從哪裡下載？

語音和語言模型會在第一次使用時從 Hugging Face 下載（每個約數百 MB）。如果你所在的地區連線 Hugging Face 較慢或無法連線，可以在同一個設定裡改用鏡像站。

如何移除 LingoGather？

結束 LingoGather，把它從「應用程式」檔案夾移到垃圾桶。你的筆記檔案夾屬於你自己，會原樣保留。

系統需求

macOS 15.6 或以上版本。Apple 內建的語音辨識需要 macOS 26 或以上版本；裝置端語言模型需要搭載 Apple 晶片的 Mac。
