LingoGather

One place on your Mac to gather what you hear and read in another language, organize it, review it and make it your own.

拾語者：在 Mac 上把聽到、讀到的外語收集起來，整理、複習，再化為己用。

拾语者：在 Mac 上把听到、读到的外语收集起来，整理、复习，再化为己用。

macOS 15.6 or later · macOS 15.6 或以上版本 · macOS 15.6 或更高版本

  • Video and audio. Play local files with dual subtitles, loop sentences, look up words and save highlights. Styled ASS subtitles, MKV and other formats work too.
  • Shadowing. Record yourself sentence by sentence and compare with the original.
  • Subtitles on your Mac. Generate subtitles with on-device speech recognition and translate them, offline with Apple Translation or with a service you choose.
  • Notes. A Markdown editor with tables, formulas and diagrams; your notes stay plain .md files in a folder you pick.
  • Flashcards. Turn notes and sentences into cards and review them with spaced repetition; desktop widgets show your streak.
  • Reading. A PDF reader with annotations, and a built-in browser with page translation and ad blocking.
  • AI practice. Chat, talk and check grammar with the AI service you connect using your own key.
