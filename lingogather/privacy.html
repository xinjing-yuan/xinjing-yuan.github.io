LingoGather Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

LingoGather is a language-learning app for macOS by xinjing. In short: we collect no data. We run no servers, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. What you create stays on your Mac and in your own iCloud. Your content goes to another company only when you have set up that company’s service with your own key and use it, and then it goes straight from your Mac to that service.

What stays on your Mac

  • Media and subtitles. Videos and audio you open are played, converted and analyzed on your Mac. Generating subtitles with on-device models or Apple’s speech recognition, translating with Apple Translation, recognizing text in PDFs and using on-device language models all happen on your Mac.
  • Microphone. LingoGather uses the microphone only while you record shadowing practice, dictate or hold a voice chat, with the permission you grant in System Settings. Recordings are saved on your Mac; audio leaves it only when you use one of the cloud services below for it.
  • Notes and study data. Notes are Markdown files in a folder you choose. Flashcards, highlights, settings and other study data are stored in LingoGather’s own sandbox container.
  • Keys. The API keys you enter are stored in your keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, they sync to your other devices through it, end-to-end encrypted by Apple.

iCloud sync

When you’re signed in to iCloud, LingoGather syncs your notes and folders, tags and comments, flashcards and review history, PDF annotations and bookmarks, subtitles, highlights and shadowing results, AI chat history, the built-in browser’s bookmarks and history, and your settings through your private iCloud database and iCloud key-value storage. PDFs and media you choose to sync are stored in LingoGather’s folder in your iCloud Drive. Apple stores this data in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy; we can’t access it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for LingoGather in System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

Services you set up yourself

LingoGather can work with the services below. Each one is used only after you enter your own key for it in Settings › API Key and choose it; services you haven’t set up are never contacted. When you use one, LingoGather sends what that request needs, together with your key, over an encrypted connection directly from your Mac to that service. Nothing goes through us, and we never see your content or your keys. The service handles the data under its own privacy policy and your agreement with it; some AI providers keep requests for a limited time, for example to detect abuse.

  • Translation — DeepL, Google Cloud Translation, Microsoft Translator, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Zhipu AI (GLM), Alibaba Cloud (Qwen) and DeepSeek receive the text you translate: subtitles, selected text, web pages, PDF pages, or text recognized in a screenshot.
  • AI chat — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Zhipu AI, Alibaba Cloud and DeepSeek receive your messages and the conversation so far, attachments you add (images, and text taken from documents), and the context you bring in: the current note, the current web page (including a video transcript or comments shown on it) or passages from the current PDF. With web search on, the provider’s own search is used. Splitting subtitles into sense groups with an AI model sends the subtitle text.
  • Voice — Voice chat streams microphone audio to OpenAI, Google Gemini or Alibaba Cloud; dictation streams it to Alibaba Cloud, OpenAI or Deepgram. Pronunciation feedback sends your shadowing recording to Microsoft Azure AI Speech, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Alibaba Cloud.
  • Reading aloud — Microsoft Azure AI Speech, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Google Gemini and Alibaba Cloud receive the text to be read.
  • Cloud subtitle generation — Deepgram, ElevenLabs and iFlytek receive the audio of the file you transcribe, and the file’s name when it is an audio file. For an online video, the video’s address may be passed to the service so that it fetches the audio itself.
  • Grammar checking — AmberPen, Sapling and Bareun receive the text you check.
  • Subtitle search — OpenSubtitles receives your search words, the languages you choose and a fingerprint (hash) of the video file, used to find matching subtitles.
  • Local model servers — Ollama and LM Studio run on your Mac by default. If you enter another address, text is sent to that address.

Other connections

  • Downloads. On-device speech and language models are downloaded from Hugging Face (or its mirror hf-mirror.com) when you download or first use them. While ad blocking is on, filter lists are updated about once a week from EasyList (easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org) and anti-AD (raw.githubusercontent.com). These requests carry nothing about you beyond what any download does, such as your IP address.
  • The built-in browser works like any browser: the websites you visit and the search engine you choose (Google, Bing, Sogou, DuckDuckGo, Perplexity or ChatGPT) receive your requests under their own policies. Cookies and website data are stored on your Mac. LingoGather also downloads websites’ icons, and previews for the bookmark cards you add to notes.
  • Online videos in notes. A YouTube, Bilibili, TED or Vimeo video you add to a note loads that site’s player whenever the note is shown. When you add a Bilibili, TED or Vimeo video, LingoGather fetches its subtitles from that site; for Bilibili it uses your Bilibili sign-in from the built-in browser.

What LingoGather doesn’t do

LingoGather has no accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t receive, sell or share any information about you.

Crash reports from Apple

If you chose to share analytics with app developers (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share with app developers), Apple may give us crash reports and anonymous usage statistics about LingoGather. Apple’s privacy policy covers that information; we use it only to fix problems.

Children

LingoGather collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@xinjing.app.

  • 翻譯——DeepL、Google Cloud Translation、Microsoft Translator、OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、智譜 AI（GLM）、阿里雲（通義千問）和 DeepSeek 會收到你要翻譯的文字：字幕、選取的文字、網頁、PDF 頁面，或從截圖中辨識出的文字。

LingoGather 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日

LingoGather（拾语者）是 xinjing 开发的 macOS 外语学习 App。简单来说：我们不收集任何数据。我们没有服务器，App 里也没有分析、广告、追踪或崩溃报告的代码。你创建的内容只留在你的 Mac 和你自己的 iCloud 里；只有在你用自己的密钥设置了某个服务并使用它时，相关内容才会从你的 Mac 直接发送给那个服务。

留在你 Mac 上的数据

  • 媒体与字幕。你打开的视频和音频都在你的 Mac 上播放、转换和分析。用本地模型或 Apple 语音识别生成字幕、用 Apple 翻译翻译、识别 PDF 里的文字、使用本地语言模型，也都在你的 Mac 上完成。
  • 麦克风。只有在你录制跟读、听写或进行语音对话时，LingoGather 才会通过你在“系统设置”中允许的权限使用麦克风。录音存在你的 Mac 上；只有当你为此使用下面的云端服务时，声音才会发送出去。
  • 笔记与学习数据。笔记是你选定文件夹里的 Markdown 文件。闪卡、重点、设置等学习数据存储在 LingoGather 自己的沙盒容器里。
  • 密钥。你填写的 API 密钥存储在你的钥匙串里。如果你使用 iCloud 钥匙串，密钥会通过它同步到你的其他设备，由 Apple 端到端加密。

iCloud 同步

你登录 iCloud 时，LingoGather 会通过你私人的 iCloud 数据库和 iCloud 键值存储，同步你的笔记与文件夹、标签与评论、闪卡与复习记录、PDF 批注与书签、字幕、重点与跟读结果、AI 对话记录、内置浏览器的书签与浏览历史，以及设置。你选择同步的 PDF 和媒体文件，会存储在你 iCloud 云盘里 LingoGather 的文件夹中。这些数据由 Apple 按其隐私政策存放在你的 iCloud 账户中，我们无法访问。若要停止同步，请在“系统设置”›“Apple 账户”›“iCloud”中关闭 LingoGather。

你自己设置的服务

LingoGather 可以配合下列服务使用。每个服务都要等你在“设置”›“API Key”中填入自己的密钥并选用它之后才会使用；你没有设置的服务，LingoGather 从不连接。当你使用某个服务时，LingoGather 会把这次请求需要的内容连同你的密钥，通过加密连接从你的 Mac 直接发送给该服务；数据不经过我们，我们也看不到你的内容和密钥。该服务按它自己的隐私政策和你与它的协议处理这些数据；部分 AI 服务会在一段时间内保留请求内容，例如用于检测滥用。

  • 翻译——DeepL、Google Cloud Translation、Microsoft Translator、OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、智谱 AI（GLM）、阿里云（通义千问）和 DeepSeek 会收到你要翻译的文字：字幕、选中的文字、网页、PDF 页面，或从截图中识别出的文字。
  • AI 对话——OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、智谱 AI、阿里云和 DeepSeek 会收到你的消息和之前的对话、你添加的附件（图片，以及从文档中提取的文字），以及你带入的内容：当前笔记、当前网页（包括页面上显示的视频字幕或评论）或当前 PDF 中的段落。开启联网搜索时，使用的是该服务自己的搜索。用 AI 模型划分意群时，会发送字幕文字。
  • 语音——语音对话会把麦克风声音实时发送给 OpenAI、Google Gemini 或阿里云；听写会发送给阿里云、OpenAI 或 Deepgram。发音反馈会把你的跟读录音发送给 Microsoft Azure AI 语音、OpenAI、Google Gemini 或阿里云。
  • 朗读——Microsoft Azure AI 语音、ElevenLabs、OpenAI、Google Gemini 和阿里云会收到要朗读的文字。
  • 云端生成字幕——Deepgram、ElevenLabs 和讯飞会收到你要转写的文件的声音；如果是音频文件，也会收到文件名。在线视频可能会把视频地址交给该服务，由它自己获取声音。
  • 语法检查——AmberPen、Sapling 和 Bareun 会收到你要检查的文字。
  • 字幕搜索——OpenSubtitles 会收到你的搜索词、你选的语言，以及视频文件的指纹（哈希值），用来找出匹配的字幕。
